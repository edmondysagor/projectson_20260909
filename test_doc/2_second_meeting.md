## Meeting Objective

Review several proposed changes to the project baseline agreed during the 2026-09-08 Kickoff Meeting.

The team discussed changes to the Project Charter and the related project timeline.

---

## 1. Charter Changes Agreed

### Change 1 — Business Objective

The original objective focused on introducing a more streamlined digital boarding process.

The team agreed that the objective should now explicitly include speed, reliability, and operational traceability.

The revised objective is:

> Improve the passenger self-service boarding experience by introducing a faster and more reliable digital boarding process, with stronger operational traceability.

---

### Change 2 — Passenger Processing Time

The team reviewed the original target of 3 seconds.

After discussion, the project target is revised to:

> Average passenger processing time should be no more than 2.5 seconds.

The previous 3-second target is therefore superseded.

---

### Change 3 — Service Availability

The original project target was 99.9% service availability.

The team agreed to raise the target to:

> 99.99% service availability.

The previous 99.9% target is therefore superseded.

---

### Change 4 — Identity Verification Method

The original scope referred generally to:

> Self-service passenger identity verification.

The team agreed that the initial solution must explicitly support two verification methods:

> QR code and facial verification.

The identity-verification scope should therefore be updated accordingly.

---

### Change 5 — Out-of-Scope Items

The team reviewed the current Out of Scope items.

The following remains out of scope:

- Replacement of the airport-wide passenger processing platform.
- Redesign of airline security-list management.
- Expansion to non-boarding airport processes.

No change is required to these items.
